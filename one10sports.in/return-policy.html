Returns & Replacement Policy

ONE10 SPORTS • Beyond Limits • Beyond Ordinary

1. Manufacturing Quality Assurance

Every piece of sportswear leaving our facility undergoes strict quality inspection for fabric strength, stitching uniformity, and color consistency.

2. Standard Retail Replacements

In the rare event of a verified manufacturing defect or transit damage in standard retail sportswear, customers must notify us within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. Upon verification, a free replacement or corrective action is initiated.

3. Custom Sublimation Teamwear

Custom-manufactured uniforms bearing player names, specific numbers, school logos, or club crests are produced upon customer design approval and cannot be returned for change-of-mind reasons. In case of printing discrepancy against the approved digital proof, corrective replacements are provided.
